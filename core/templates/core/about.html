About Us

Rental Platform is a simple web application built with Django and Bootstrap to help property owners and tenants connect with each other.

Who we are

We are a small student project made to practice full stack web development. Our goal is to make renting a property easier for both owners and tenants, by giving them one place to list properties, send booking requests, make payments and raise maintenance tickets.

What we offer

  • Easy property listing for owners
  • Simple booking requests for tenants
  • Basic payment and maintenance tracking
  • Separate dashboards for owners, tenants and admins
